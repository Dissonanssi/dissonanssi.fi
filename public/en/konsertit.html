Christmas Concert 2025 7.12

Mixed choir Dissonanssi offers listeners a refined selection of Christmas- and wintertime music in our concert Wintery Passage. We sing choral works from beautiful, harmonious hymns to merry English carols, without forgetting Finnish composers, either. A mixture of calm and energetic songs is sure to bring a moment of rest in the busy, dark days preceding Christmas. Wolcum!

Tickets: 10€/16€
Time: 7.12 18:00
Location: Pitäjänmäen kirkko, Turkismiehenkuja 4

Dissonanssi concerts

Dissonanssi organises two concerts during the year, one during the spring semester and one during the fall semester. In addition to these, you can see us perform in some other events, for example Kuorojen kierros during Art Goes Kapakka. We post information of upcoming gigs in Facebook!
